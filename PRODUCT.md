# Blackout

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Confirmed master plan: TypeScript, React, Phaser 4, Web Worker simulation, local Node server, Zod, IndexedDB, Vitest, Playwright.

## Users
New data-center staff practicing incident command. A solo builder demonstrates the local application to hackathon judges.

## Product Purpose
Connect timestamped evidence, team decisions, and computed service/thermal consequences through one complete Hot Restart mission.

## Operating Context
Local laptop browser, eight racks in one hall, two field technicians and a remote IT operator. Guided mission, replay and one earlier-batch-cap comparison.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Deterministic one-second simulation. Observations remain separate from truth. Grounded OpenAI coworkers use validated tools. The entire mission works without a provider. No live equipment connection, public hosting, multiplayer, login or certification.

## Brand Commitments
Approved flat overhead, visibly coarse pixel art. Orthogonal 16-pixel grid; 320 by 192 logical viewport. Dark industrial rooms, cyan indicators, restrained purple and amber details. Preserve this direction.

## Evidence on Hand
The supplied master plan and repository documentation seed. No supplied runtime artwork or calibrated facility data. Models and procedures are fictional training assumptions.

## Product Principles
The engine owns outcomes. Evidence includes timestamps. Every intervention has a cost and prerequisite. Fallback is explicit. One complete causal incident precedes expansion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard equipment list, readable DOM text, visible focus, textual states, reduced motion, adequate contrast and responsive inspector.
